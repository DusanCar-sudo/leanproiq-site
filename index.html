    Where it pays

    Hotels, workshops and factories with motors and compressors, on a business supply of 40 kW or more. Set the power factor the site has today; the node corrects it to 0.95.

    Reactive-power charge removed

    Share of the active-energy bill that the supplier adds while the power factor is below 0.90. At 0.90 or better it is zero.

    Less line current

    The same work drawn through the same cables and transformer, with capacity freed for more load.

    53.2%

    Less cable heating loss

    Resistive loss in the supply branch falls with the square of the current.

    Predictions from the engineering model, not measurements. The charge follows the table in Vietnam's Circular 15/2014/TT-BCT (k = 0.9 / cosφ − 1); confirm the current rule and the site's contract before quoting it. Current and loss figures assume the same real power before and after.

    How it saves

    Measure, decide, act

    1

    Measure

    Real power, reactive power, power factor and harmonics, per circuit, all day.

    2

    Decide

    The agent spots idle loads, peak-tariff overlap and poor power factor, and plans the cheapest safe response.

    3

    Act

    It delays, staggers or cuts loads and engages capacitor stages, then logs what it did and why.
